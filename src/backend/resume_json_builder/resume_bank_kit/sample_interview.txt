[project_forecast] Campus Demand Forecast was a completed course project from January through April 2026. We were a team of three. I personally implemented the ridge-regression baseline, chronological data split, and leakage checks in Python, NumPy, and scikit-learn. A teammate implemented the neural-network model. We used 24 months of synthetic hourly demand data supplied by the instructor. I chose chronological rather than random splitting to avoid using future data in training. The last four months were the held-out test set. My ridge baseline had a mean absolute error of 12.4 demand units; the same test set gave 15.8 for the seasonal-naive baseline. The figures describe synthetic course data, not business results. I validated the split with assertions and manually inspected feature timestamps. Initially, a rolling feature included the current target; I shifted it before evaluation. We submitted a class report and demonstration. Nothing was deployed. The public demo is at https://example.com/projects/campus-demand.
